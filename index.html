About this portal

The RSPB publishes geospatial data to support evidence-based conservation. Data are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence unless stated otherwise. This site is a community-built front end backed by the RSPB’s ArcGIS Hub catalogue; the underlying datasets, services and metadata are owned and maintained by the RSPB.

Map tiles and rendering are provided by Felt.
